=== WHY THIS POINT AND NO OTHER ===

E27 named the host latch and E28 closed the guest half. The park is produced by
exactly one statement — `waitExternal(EeWaitReason::Mpeg, kMpegPictureWaitType,
…)` at MPEG.cpp:2514 — reached only through the four-term guard at 2498–2501.
Of those four terms, `!playback.streamEnded` is the ONLY one that (a) the host
already sets on two real paths (`finishPlaybackStream` :1459 and the
kMpegProgramEnd path :1303), (b) the guest's own player already handles, and
(c) does not require inventing a state the runtime cannot otherwise be in.
Setting it at the top of the wait block — AFTER the producer dispatch at 2489 —
is what makes the bypass BOUNDED: the flag-on run reproduces the faithful run
byte-for-byte up to and including the 5,040-byte parser feed, and diverges only
at the park.

Rejected alternatives, and why:
  * Wire P7's `ssx3MoviePlayComplete` via ps2_call_list.h + ssx3.toml. Needs a
    RE-RECOMP of the 9,454 generated guest sources; the brief permits exactly
    two worktree mutations and regeneration is not one of them. P7 also says
    its hook address is not runtime-validated, and E27/E28 have since located
    the real call path precisely.
  * Suppress the `nonStreamDeliveries` latch (erase the key / skip the weak_ptr).
    That is a candidate FIX for E30's question, not a bypass. It would burn the
    E30 experiment on an unmeasured guess and is out of scope by the brief.
  * Return a synthetic decoded frame. "Replace arbitrary waits with success",
    explicitly ruled out by the review. It would also make the flag-on run
    uninformative about what lies past the park.
  * Flush the decoder when the flag arms (`flushDecoderIfEnded`). Could emit a
    frame from the 5,040 partial bytes and change picturesServed; enlarges the
    blast radius for no reachability gain.
