A scan of all 922 GIF packets recorded at ticks 949 and 950 found one
sprite packet per tick, both spanning the entire 512x448 display; the other
packets' decoded XYZ positions did not isolate the observed broken glyph
region. This packet is a display-area/composite candidate, NOT a proven
per-glyph source. The capture cannot attribute a broken glyph to a unique
primitive or texture upload from these fields alone. A packet-level pixel
trace or texture-source probe is needed before naming a glyph producer.
