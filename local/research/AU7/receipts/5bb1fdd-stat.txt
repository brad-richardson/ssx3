    [AU2] SND spike: PS2X_SND_TICK delivers SNDDRV's cid-1 tick to the EE handler; logs cid-0/SetDma/RPC
    
    Dev-only, default off. With PS2X_SND_TICK=<n>, each vblank queues n
    Interrupt invocations of the cid-1 handler registered via
    sceSifAddCmdHandler (packet type 0, opt = SNDDRV's tag-buffer IOP
    address 0xB3C4) and stamps the status serial at the init RPC's status
    address. cid-0 type-1 (dmqueue) requests are logged, their staged
    payload dumped, and answered with a type-2 completion. SND-library
    sceSifSetDma transfers are captured host-side instead of being copied
    into low EE RDRAM. While on, the 0x426078 (_sceSifSendCmd) entry is
    parsed with its shifted argument layout. Suite 597/597.
