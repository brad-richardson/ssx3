   `hwIntc` handling) and any HLE of the handler invocation.
2. **Alarm stack.** `SetAlarm` callbacks: what context and whose stack

3. **CD callback thread.** `sceCdInitEeCB` / `sceCdCallback`: stack
   address and size, owning thread, and where PCSX2 models it. Compare
   `g_cdCallbackStackTop` and the parked-thread finding (P1 Part 6).
4. **Async / SIF RPC callbacks.** Where do SIF RPC server threads and
   other async callbacks get stacks in PCSX2? Compare
   `reserveAsyncCallbackStack` (`ps2_runtime.cpp`) and its region.
5. **EE kernel reserved area.** What does PCSX2 (or its BIOS/HLE docs)
   say the `0x00000000-0x000fffff` kernel region holds, and is any of
   it free for a runtime to borrow? Cite, do not assume.
