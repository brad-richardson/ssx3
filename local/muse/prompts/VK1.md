# VK1 — present straight from Vulkan (option B): Odin-first scoping + prototype (Opus spike, ≤ 8 h)

## Goal
Today every platform copies the finished game image out of Vulkan into raylib's GL/GLES texture (zero-copy exists only on Apple, HR1). On the Odin that copy is a GPU→CPU readback plus a GLES upload every frame, and F5 measured **4×+hi-res at −31 %** (0.168× vs 0.244× race). Brad (09-25) approved a spike on option B: present with Vulkan directly. Answer three questions with evidence: (1) how much of the Odin's 4× cost is the readback/upload path vs GPU fill vs Turnip driver CPU; (2) what B takes per platform (Odin, Mac, iOS); (3) does an Odin prototype work, match pixels, and how fast is it at 1× and 4×?
