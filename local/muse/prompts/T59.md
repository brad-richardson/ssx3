## Why (orchestrator read of T58)

T58's store watch folds addresses with `& 0x1FFFFFFF`. The EE's RAM
aliases are:
- `0x00000000` (kuseg);
- `0x20000000` (uncached);
- `0x30000000` (uncached-accelerated, "UCAB");
- `0x80000000` / `0xA0000000` (kseg0/1).

The fold maps `0x30809670` to `0x10809670`, so a UCAB store never matches.
SSX 3 writes DMA-bound data through UCAB (`sub_00362978` ORs `0x30000000`
into its store address). So "TLB-mapped" (T58 §5) most likely means "UCAB
store the watch couldn't see". The recomp maps UCAB to RAM correctly
(`ps2_memory.h:128-141`).

## Change (on T58's tree)

In the store hook, resolve RAM aliases to physical before matching:
- if the address is in `0x00`, `0x20`, `0x30`, `0x80` or `0xA0` segments
  and below the segment base + 32 MiB, use `addr & 0x01FFFFFF`;
- otherwise leave it unmatched.

Log the **original** virtual address as `vaddr=` in `ebw` lines. Keep
everything else.
