## Why

The stock race runs on the Mac, but its 3D world renders near-black (HUD
only; no rider, sky or terrain textures), and Select Character lacks the
3D Zoe model. VU1 is a cycle-stepped interpreter; the VIF1 MSCAL/MSCNT
callbacks (`ps2xRuntime/src/lib/ps2_runtime.cpp` ~753–788) call
`m_vu1.execute/resume(…, 65536)` and `run()` (`vu/ps2_vu1_core.cpp`
~1625–1660) **stops silently when the budget is spent**; nothing re-enters
a cut-off program. XGKICK output goes out as PATH1. There is no draw export.

Hypotheses and the observable that separates them (per guest vsync, at
Select Character settled and in-race):
- **H1 budget truncation:** `vu1_budget_exhausted > 0`, and PATH1 draws are
  few relative to MSCALs. Correct-behavior model: every MSCAL program
  reaches its E-bit (exhausted = 0).
- **H2 GS-side:** exhausted = 0, PATH1 draws plentiful, pixels still missing.
- **H3 VU1 math/unpack:** exhausted = 0, PATH1 draws plentiful, but draw
  bounding boxes degenerate/off-screen (log xyz min/max per path).
T48 (PCSX2, running on bytesize) brings the healthy numbers; your table
must stand on its own for H1 either way.
